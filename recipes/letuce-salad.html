Letuce Salad Recipe

Description/Recipes

Letuce salad is a delicious and easy-to-make dish that is perfect for any occasion.

It is crunchy and has alot of flavors that pairs well with rice, pasta, or bread.

Ingredients

  • Letuce
  • tomatoe
  • pepper
  • onions
    • >
  • Shredded beef
  • Seasoning
  • Grinded kuli kuli
  • Odin Recipes

Steps

  1. Wash and slice letuce then keep aside.
  2. Wash and then slice onions,pepper and tomatoes.
  3. Pour the letuce into a bowl and then mix with sliced vegetables.
  4. Add up the shredded meat and kuli kuli into the mixture.
  5. Mix everything together until well combined then serve.
