Jollof Recipe

Description/Recipes

Jollof rice is a universal dish which is very much well known across Africa.

Jollof rice is a meal that contains alot of flavours that gives joy to our taste buds.

It contains various recipes such as African spices,chicken,beef,fish,rice and tomatoes as the main ingredient.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Pepper and Onions
  • Seasoning
  • Spice
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken/Beef
  • Odin Recipes

Steps

  1. Wash and rinse the rice,then keep aside.
  2. Wash and blend tomatoes,pepper and onions.
  3. Heat oil in the pot and fry the blended tomatoe paste.
  4. When properly fried,add seasoning and spices of choice.
  5. Add washed rice into the pot of fried paste and add some water.
  6. Mix properly and cover with foil to steem well,then allow to cook.
  7. Cook or fry your chicken,beef or fish then serve with the jollof rice.
