Egg Sauce Recipe

Description/Recipes

Egg sauce is a delicious and easy-to-make dish that is perfect for any occasion.

It is a creamy and flavorful sauce that pairs well with rice, pasta, or bread.

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • tomatoe
  • pepper
  • onions
  • Vegetable oil
  • Hot dogs
  • Shrimps
  • Shredded beef
  • Seasoning
  • Odin Recipes

Steps

  1. Fry tomatoe paste in a pan.
  2. Gradually sliced onions,hot dog shrinmos and shredded beef.
  3. Stir the mixture to a fry and at the same time cook.
  4. Crack eggs into a separate bowl and beat them.
  5. Slowly pour the beaten eggs into the sauce while stirring continuously.
  6. Season with salt, pepper, and any other desired spices.
  7. Serve hot with yam,potatoe chips,french fries or even bread.
